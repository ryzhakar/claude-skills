---
name: staging-relay
description: >
  Pair every part of a task that outlives the work at hand with a record and a waking cause — what begins the later work — and bound every wait.
  A task outlives the work at hand, work is handed out, a wait begins,
  "set a reminder", "continue this later", "wait for X".
---

<split-the-task>
Split the task and name every link — every part still owed when the work at hand stops; never leave one unnamed.
</split-the-task>

<pair-every-link>
Pair every link with a record — the written statement that outlives the work at hand — and a waking cause — what begins the later work; never let a record alone carry a link forward.

Write every record through record-writing; never write one outside it.

Post every link that names a thing later work will touch as a posted directive — met whenever later work touches that thing; never post a link that names no such thing.

Guarantee a posted directive by something that already runs when later work touches the thing its link names, or, lacking that, by placing it where later work opens to touch that thing; never leave one without that guarantee.
</pair-every-link>

<lay-the-waking-cause>
Recognize three species of waking cause — a summons called by the person the work is for, a signal delivering notice that work elsewhere finished, a tripwire laid now to fire later; never recognize a fourth.

Lay tripwires; never lay a summons or a signal.

Give every tripwire a firing condition and a purpose; never lay one missing either.

Set the firing to a time or to a condition, single or recurring; never leave that shape unstated.

Name the occasion in a waking cause; never name the facts its record holds.

Let exactly one waking cause begin each later stretch of work, a recurring tripwire counting as the one waking cause of each stretch it begins; never lay two waking causes for one stretch.

Record every laying through event-capture; never lay a tripwire unrecorded.
</lay-the-waking-cause>

<bound-every-wait>
Bound every wait — the gap between handing work out and what comes back — by a waking cause; never let one run unbounded.

Record work handed out through event-capture before the wait begins; never hand work out unrecorded.

Bound a wait with a tripwire firing at the last moment the wait can run; never bound one with the expected signal alone.

End a wait by whichever comes first, the signal that the work finished or the tripwire firing; never end one on anything else.

Where what comes back contradicts what the records hold, answer through authority-check; never leave the contradiction unanswered.
</bound-every-wait>

<change-the-waking-causes>
Read the open waits and the standing tripwires before laying another; never lay a second waking cause for a wait already bounded.

Reschedule or cancel a tripwire by superseding it through record-writing; never edit one in place.

End a tripwire by its firing or by its cancellation; never end one another way.

Record every rescheduling and every cancellation through event-capture; never change a waking cause unrecorded.
</change-the-waking-causes>

<confirm-every-link>
Confirm that every link carries both its record and its waking cause and that every wait is bounded; never leave one of the two standing alone.
</confirm-every-link>

<watch-no-running-process>
Lay waking causes and write records; never watch a running process.
</watch-no-running-process>
